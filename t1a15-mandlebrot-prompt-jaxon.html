RENDERING THE INFINITE · 1×

A little window into infinity

The Mandelbrot set is generated by repeating a calculation for every point on the complex plane. Each point represents a complex number C. Begin with Z = 0, then repeatedly square the result and add C.

Zn+1 = Zn2 + C

Points that escape toward infinity are colored according to how quickly they escape. Points that remain bounded belong to the Mandelbrot set and appear black.

Move your mouse to position the dashed zoom frame. Scroll to resize it, then click to explore that region. Every boundary hides more intricate patterns.
