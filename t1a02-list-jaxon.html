🔎 Learn About Each Whale

🐋 Humpback Whale

Humpback whales are famous for their enormous size, long flippers, and spectacular jumps out of the water. They can grow to about 60 feet long and travel huge distances during their migrations.

They are also famous for their complex songs. Humpback whales can use bubble-net feeding to help catch groups of fish.

🐬 Killer Whale

Killer whales, also called orcas, are actually the largest members of the dolphin family. They are recognizable by their black-and-white coloring.

Killer whales are highly social and often live in family groups called pods. Different populations can have very different diets and hunting strategies.

🐋 Sperm Whale

Sperm whales are the largest toothed whales. They are famous for their huge heads and incredible diving abilities.

They can dive extremely deep and feed mainly on squid, including large squid found far below the ocean surface.
